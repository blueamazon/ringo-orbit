Easy to start. Hard to master.

All you need is one finger. Every hit makes the next one harder.

  1. Time your tap

    Tap when your orb lines up with the yellow target. Miss, and your run is over.

  2. Chain perfects

    Land three dead-centre hits to trigger Hyper Fever and earn double points.

  3. Survive the speed-up

    More rings, a ticking clock and red hazards all arrive as your score climbs.

Two ways to play

Start with Orbit, then earn Breaker. Each has its own feel and its own best score to beat.

Your orb circles the core. Lunge at the target as the rings spin around you.

  • 45 third ring

🧱 Breaker

Drag the bar, bounce the ball and smash the bricks. Catch falling perks like Ball Multiplier, Fireball and Laser Bar.

  • 5 waves to each boss
