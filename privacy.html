Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026

This policy explains what data Ringo Orbit (the "app") handles.

Data stored on your device

Your scores, collected items, skins, missions and settings are saved on your device. We don't have accounts and we don't receive this data. If Android backup is turned on, your device may include it in your own Google backup.

Reset Progress in Settings deletes your scores, collected items, skins and missions. Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on the device.

Advertising

The app can show rewarded video ads through Google AdMob when you choose to watch one. Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

If you are in a region where consent is required, such as the EEA or the UK, the app asks for your choice before showing ads. You can change it at any time under Ad Privacy Choices in Settings.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings.

Sharing your score

If you tap Share, the text goes only to the app you choose. We don't see it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new effective date.
